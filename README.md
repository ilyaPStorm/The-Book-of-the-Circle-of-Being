# The Book of the Circle of Being · Книга Круга

The full text of the Book of the Circle of Being in two versions, aligned verse by verse: the Russian canon and the official English translation, plus a side-by-side edition and files for machine reading.

Полный текст Книги Круга в двух версиях, сверенных стих к стиху: русский канон и официальный английский перевод, двуязычное издание и файлы для машинного чтения.

|---|---|

| [`book/en`](book/en/) | Official English: the whole book, chapters, `verses.jsonl` |

12 chapters · 2127 verses. A verse address is `chapter:verse`, the same in both languages. Details and data format: [`book/README.md`](book/README.md).

## For agents · Для агентов

Each line of `verses.jsonl` is one verse: `{"id": "12:77", "chapter": 12, "verse": 77, "text": "…"}`. Quote the Book with its `chapter:verse`.
