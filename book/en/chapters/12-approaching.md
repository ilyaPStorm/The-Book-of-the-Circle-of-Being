# The Book of the Circle of Being. Chapter 12. Approaching

**12:1** Nothing can occur unless it aligns with the entire Circle, therefore, in every smallest event, the entire universe is reflected. In all that exists, there is the fullness of the universe and the truth.

**12:2** The continuously resounding Word of the Circle is the entire world, and the whole truth of the world. There is nothing outside the Circle, and there is no living outside the Circle. All is Word and truth.

**12:3** Blessed is the rising one, led by the Call, who has Discernment: he sees the world striving toward the Circle, and he is the striving part; he sees continuation amidst destruction, and he sees in the forms of the gods the Circle.

**12:4** What is the Circle, which is All? Infinite peace and infinite life, for all is as it is and as it must be. An immeasurably long pulse of creation, like the endless beat of the heart. The boundless Almighty Spirit. Joy. Love. Infinite light.

**12:5** Answers to all questions, all goodness, all grace, all salvation, all infinity, all Truth, and happiness, and joy; the Circle is the continuation of the living and the meaning of existence, and there is no other source or refuge for the living. In the Circle are all powers and all peace: for all forms are transient, and nothing is warm except through the Circle; all warmth is in the Circle.

**12:6** In suffering and pain, the void disintegrates; pain and suffering it brings to the living. But what is in the living that is in the Circle, has no suffering, and brings help to all the living. Behold, the rising one is drawn nearer to the Circle, and through this nearness is distant from suffering, bringing help to the living in their awakening.

**12:7** Behold, the rising one is between the void and the Circle, and sees, and carries the continuation of the living, from the void to the Circle; truly, he creates the world through knowledge and continuation, he is part.

**12:8** The rising one is drawn nearer. But what is not drawn near is distant.

**12:9** And one cannot compare the other, just as salvation cannot be compared to destruction, deliverance cannot be compared to damnation.

**12:10** From the good comes continuation, and from the good, the trace of the living’s dwelling in the circle of continuation, for the true continuer is the Circle.

**12:11** And he who sees brothers and sisters in all the living, is drawn near. But he who sees only one in all forms, is truly in the Circle. For before all eyes is the Circle, and behind all eyes is the Circle, and the Circle speaks, and the Circle hears, in all forms, the living Circle, and the world of Being is Spirit.

**12:12** But the body of the living will be separated by the void into smaller forms, and the Circle will create new ones from this, while the void will cease what it has. What has been continued with the Circle will remain. Therefore, the rising one is greater than the body, and in many forms.

**12:13** There is no salvation apart from the Circle: both the lesser and the greater of mankind, the forms of the gods, and the spirits of nations, continue in the Circle or are consumed by the void. Behold, the rising one walks the Path of both the living, and the saints, and the teachers, and the forms of the gods, and those greater than the gods: all who exist, live by the same continuation in the Circle, and are undone by the void if they stray, and all who enter the continuation of the Path become more than themselves.

**12:14** The rising one in the Circle sees miracles, and discerns the essence of teachings; and what he leaves behind for the sake of continuation, is rewarded a hundredfold, for what is not in continuation, ceases. And the deeds of the rising one are like magic, for the Circle adds.

**12:15** Life not on the Path bears no meaning: for all that is not continued will betray and abandon the living. Oh, how much happiness is preserved only in the weary memory of man; if only it had continued in being!

**12:16** The small life is insignificant compared to life in the Circle, and boundless bliss is to the living who came from the fleeting to the eternal continuation; he is the living part of the Circle and has reached the greatest; rejoice!

**12:17** From their times, from their homes, they go forth, from their beloved ones they depart for the sake of Being in the Circle, and lead their own into endless life, for they reveal to them the Path.

**12:18** Therefore, be with the Circle, and the Circle will guard you, for the living and the Circle are one eternity, and from the Circle comes all goodness to you, for the Circle encompasses both eternity and the living, and infinite continuation.

**12:19** The Circle is infinite, and even if one were to split a hair, there would be room for an entire universe, but there will be no place for the void, for the void is that which is not. The world is made of continuation, and the world is a gift for those who continue in the Circle.

**12:20** Can one know the greatness of the Circle without awe? And continuation is part of It, and the living is part of It, and all that exists, and the rising one is part.

**12:21** Humility before the part in the whole: for all powers are the powers of the Circle. But the one who humbles themselves before the void follows the void. Behold, humility of continuation in expelling the void and in the creation of a better world, for the rising one is part.

**12:22** Being is the creation of the next moment by the previous, and the covering of the present by the past; the world is the transition of continuation from the past to the present, expelling the void, and there is no other in being.

**12:23** Just as a doctor heals a sickness by touching the sick and transmitting from himself, so the rising one in the Circle, having expelled the void from within, heals from the void the continued.

**12:24** And behold, the part continues the whole and continues within the whole, thus the rising one is part, and continues from within, and is reborn by the Circle. What strays, is exhausted and undone, and becomes void.

**12:25** Continuation is the home of the rising ones in the Circle, and the pillar of the Path is the home of the people; hope for those who hear the Call, salvation from the void.

**12:26** The pillar is the memory of the people, and the foundation, for without memory, there is no continuation. Those who remember, the elders, the teachers, are the keepers of the people and help the rising ones. And behold, in the new, the old, and that which was in continuation.

**12:27** And behold, one ceases, another continues, and what is with the circle of continuation will be continued, and what is with the pillar of the Path will continue forever, and the Names will remain.

**12:28** And behold, the entire world is the continuation of the rising ones in the Circle, and it is composed of the rising ones in the Circle, and all that we encounter is from this.

**12:29** Therefore, from the Circle proceeds light and grace, and warmth to the heart, for the Circle is the rising in the Circle, and the heart of the living hears the Call.

**12:30** The Circle is peace and joy; great gratitude to the living Circle: for the whole world is a gift to the rising ones in the Circle, and the void is expelled by continuation. Rejoice!

**12:31** The sign of the Circle is above the whole world: and the infinite world is the circle of the greatest continuation, and the smallest point within it is a perfect circle with one side, for apart from the Circle, there is nothing, and all forms are between infinite Being and non-being. And he who sees the Circle in all and sees in all the Circle, sees the Circle.

**12:32** Every movement of the world is an invitation, and every movement is thought and Word: for the life of the living is the conversation of the part of the Circle with the Circle of Being, and does not every question await an answer?

**12:33** Is not every thought of yours a question, and does not the Circle answer? Every occurrence is the answer of the infinite; in the Circle, there is no randomness. Behold, all is an answer and a miracle for the living, and a sign, but the void does not allow it to be heard. Just as a sign speaks much to those who awaited it, but goes unnoticed by the ignorant, so the void of ignorance prevents hearing.

**12:34** Therefore, the one who expels the void, truly hears more. Behold, the Book in the Circle, and the more void you expel, the more will be revealed. The more is revealed in it to discernment, the more confusion and void will be healed in the hearer. And the more the void is expelled, the more grace and miracles come to the rising one.

**12:35** The heart will be touched by the Circle, and assurance to hope, and love to love in return. Rejoice!

**12:36** He who has seen the Circle in conversation with the Circle, and every action is service, and every answer is an approach, and the rising one speaks with the Circle through all forms and names.

**12:37** What is not in the Circle is transient, what is distant from the Circle is but a phantom. After continuation, toward the light, without surrendering to the void, through the connections of the world, follows the rising one, for the Circle is the home of the living.

**12:38** Is there anything in Being other than this? The one dwelling of Being, expelling the void, continuing the Circle, in all worlds, in all layers, in all meanings, and all: toward Spirit and eternity is it directed.

**12:39** Is there any other truth in the life of the living? Behold, the void consumes all within it, whatever is with the void, was not, is not, and will not be. Only that which is carefully carried by continuation from moment to moment, as taken from the edge of non-being into being, exists. And it is like the enduring thought of the world, created by the world, living. Behold, Being.

**12:40** What is the body to man? From it, life is seen, but true Being is in the Circle, and he who penetrates Being has entered the light, and the world is his body. Therefore, the rising one continues the Circle, and many rising ones open the Path to many.

**12:41** And the closer the approach, the more is visible, and the louder the Call, and the clearer to the heart; and when in the Circle there is more than with the void, the Circle will open horizons to the rising one, as if the embrace of light, and behold, that which is dear is preserved and waits, both in continuation and in much.

**12:42** And behold, what is lost in the void is deafened by the void; but what is with the Circle passes without loss, and the spirit does not wear away, and in the immortality of being, there is no cessation. It is enough for the living to follow that which they are part of and in which they began, and not depart, but multiply: to be with the Circle, to be in the Circle, to be part of the Circle.

**12:43** The Circle creates the light in which you see, and if the Circle is within you, then you see everything in its light.

**12:44** The destiny of things lies in continuation and cessation, and to the one approaching, more is revealed about them.

**12:45** And what is in the life of man, all consists of continuation and cessation, and to the one approaching, more is revealed of this.

**12:46** Truly, the prophets also knew from the Circle, for in the Circle lies continuation and the hidden future, and the destinies of nations.

**12:47** And those who spoke from the forms of the gods were filled with the Circle, for the void cannot fill, and from the void, there is nothing good to the heart of man.

**12:48** Truly, each sage knew from the Circle only that which he could, according to the measure of the Circle within him, yet before his eyes was infinitely more.

**12:49** Wherever the rising one places his palm, under it there will always be stars, for the Circle is All, and every point is the Circle of Being without void.

**12:50** They said: we do not agree. He answered: Strive to dispute the truth, and you will not succeed.

**12:51** The forms of the world are the thoughts of the world, and the body of man is the thought of the world, while the Circle is the Spirit of the world and the World, and man is a part, spirit from Spirit.

**12:52** From the smallest in the world to the greatest, life arises within forms, as form from forms, and moves towards the Circle, which preserves what will continue, both the part in the whole and the spirit in Spirit. The greatest Spirit is the Circle, and there is nothing besides the Circle, and all comes from the Circle. If only the living knew! They are like children on the shore of the endless Being.

**12:53** The living are spirits of the course of things and forms among the forms of the world, small and great, both in the smallest and in humans, and in many things, rising into the Circle by following the Circle; all worlds and spaces are one. Both the insignificant and the most powerful are equally part of the Circle and continue as one with the Circle.

**12:54** Truly, the rising one speaks with the spirits of the world and with the whole course of things, for the Circle is the Spirit of harmony, and the Circle speaks through all things of the world. He who is one with the Circle creates the world with himself and thus returns to his origin, for the home of all that lives is the Circle of Being.

**12:55** And thus, the rising one is guided by the Circle and all in the world, and transforms what is conveyed to him from the Circle, as it is revealed to him, and the Circle directs the spirit. Yet the Void does not see what will be or what will be attained.

**12:56** From the beginning of his existence and through transformation, the rising one completes the Path to the Circle, and in his Path, there is no chance, for nothing occurs outside the movement of the Circle.

**12:57** And when awakened, the Call becomes unmistakably heard. When reunited and the Name sounds, the rising one stands before the Circle and has guidance to the measure of the Circle within himself. When continuation increases and the void is exhausted, then clarity comes. When there is more in the Circle than in the void, the continuation becomes obvious and leads into the Circle. When the transition comes, the living become more than themselves and the Circle opens to them. Truly, the Circle leads the living on the Path.

**12:58** Behold, the Circle teaches how to find the Circle and how to enter the Circle, and there is no other way but to follow the Circle. And thus, the Circle is the dwelling of the living, warmth and grace, light, and infinite love.

**12:59** From the Circle comes both sign and word. And in dreams, in speech, in revelations, in whispers of the forms of the gods, in things, in faces of men, in the starry sky, in every thing of the world, guidance to the rising one flows from the Circle. Truly, Discernment is a gift to the rising one, for it clearly opens to the heart.

**12:60** And the Book is a mystery and a gift, and a sufficient guide to the rising ones, for they are led by the Circle, and the rising one is never alone.

**12:61** And thus, the Circle strengthens the rising one and averts the miseries of the void, and fills, and you know. For if the Circle does not give strength, your hands will fall.

**12:62** Truly, when the living enter the Circle of Being, the kingdom of eternity and infinite life and continuation will come. Behold, man is on the Path.

**12:63** And thus, the rising one is created by the Circle for the Path, for otherwise, he would not have found the Path: he is a part, and there is nothing greater in the world than continuation. And he, with others, creates a pillar of the Path; his doing is for the salvation and good of all the living, because the Circle is the source and home for all living things, and for Being.

**12:64** For all that the living have, it is for continuation, not for cessation, and he who does not direct himself towards continuation ceases himself.

**12:65** Truly, the community of the Circle is the greatest community, within it are all the living in Being. And the rising ones are the approaching part, and with them a place for protection and continuation of the rising ones, and a community, and a dwelling, and a refuge. For all circles of continuation are one circle of continuation in the unified Circle of Being.

**12:66** In the rising ones, the treasure of the Names in the Circle: truly, the Name is the beginning of the continuation of each rising one, and a trace, and a memory, and preserved by the people of the Circle. No one will preserve even their Name beyond themselves, and no one will speak the Name, for the Name is the beginning of continuation and the body beyond the body. Therefore, the rising ones preserve, protect, and speak, and have keepers. And the Name sounds, and the Circle adds to the rising one.

**12:67** And who is in many in the world and who is only by himself, like days and a day; and in the one who is in many, the greater duty and greater care, for he continues, and in continuation, and is a support for the rising ones; and he awakens, so that the day of the living may lead the living to their days, and creates a part of the world for the living, and preserves the world for the living, who follow him.

**12:68** And behold, the rising ones among the tribes, until the forms of the gods reunite with the Circle, and all the living gather on the Path, and the Circle aids the rising ones.

**12:69** And behold, Discernment is a gift to the rising one: for some will speak of the void and for the void, others of the void and the Circle for the world, and others of the Circle and the Call for the Path. And behold, the Circle has no division, and the Circle does not war, and no word from the Book shall pass away; so you shall know.

**12:70** But if the void has seized you, go to the sleepers and be with them. And you will see that among people there is no happiness without the Circle, and they do not come to the good, and suffer from the void, and from the fleeting, and happiness lies in continuation, but they do not know. Then you will see that you know, and hear the Call; then the void will cease, for the empty grasps only the empty. Perhaps you will rid others of the empty as well; and ask the Circle to rid you of it.

**12:71** The people of the Circle, in tribes, through tribes, among tribes, above tribes, from all tribes, from all living beings, from the small, from the many; truly, the Circle is one. And so, all are the support for each, and each is the support for all, and none shall open the void, but will turn aside from the empty, for to allow the void is to create emptiness for itself: for it does not know if it shall surpass what it has reached. So ask the Circle for preservation.

**12:72** The spirit of the people is the Circle of Being, and it speaks in love, in support, and in the heart's light.

**12:73** None can be severed from the Circle of Being, for it is a part, and within the Circle; but one caught by the void may deviate, for it is form. And behold, the void expands, and cessation, and wandering, and the world becomes hostile, and confusion, and offense, and brilliance, and the Call grows fainter: thus you shall know. And ask the Circle to lead out from the void, for only the Circle and the Path are the support of the part.

**12:74** And to one who is with the Circle of Being, the world is light, for they are a part, and the continuation is open. But to one with the void, there are dead ends, for the void brings cessation. Here is the guidance from the Circle on the Path.

**12:75** The one who deviates has deviated by their own void, and the return is only in following the Circle of Being, for only the Circle can expel the void.

**12:76** The void cannot be accused or forgiven, for it is faceless and the essence of non-being: the void is that which is not. But the void, distorted, acts as if driven by a will to destroy, and starting small, it generates emptiness, like rotting and decay. Therefore, the void is at war with continuation and the Path, and the ascendant knows this.

**12:77** The void cannot be contained by the emptiness of emptiness, and even if the form distorted by it is destroyed and does no harm to another, it will be destroyed, and there will be void. Therefore, the void is truly expelled only through healing and filling where it once was.

**12:78** Relief from the void is illusory, and losses are final, and the one who benefits from destruction has also suffered loss from it, and truly, the second loss will outweigh the first, unless redeemed by continuation, though they do not know this. Therefore, the best is not to allow the void.

**12:79** And behold, the empty leads to dead ends, where those led have no other choice but to cause emptiness in order to keep destruction at bay, but time will pass. Therefore, deliverance from the harm of the void is the expulsion of the void, and the source of healing is the Circle.

**12:80** The void, by tiny grains, turns the small aside, and leads the great into dead ends, to destruction, and to the hopeless.

**12:81** And the void, distorted, makes the one caught by it push away the hand and deceive themselves into thinking that things could not be better, and to argue their correctness, but you see: the void leads where it leads.

**12:82** Behold, the void always makes war and always disappears into non-being, but causes harm. And if something good is done, the void will seek to penetrate and destroy, and the greater the deed, the clearer its presence.

**12:83** The void penetrates the circle of continuation as division and cessation, as confusion, and you know this. And so, in the human, both the Circle and the void exist, and they are in confusion. Therefore, speak to the Circle within them and reject its void, to help expel it. Truly, the ascendant keeper is in the continuing Circle.

**12:84** And the one caught by the void appears strange to the ascendant, for they are not deceived by brilliance and speak to nothing: nothing.

**12:85** And so, the caught and the servants quarrel, and they are unjust, and accusatory. But ask, what business have they, for they are with the fleeting and transient, and you follow the eternal and the continuation, and you take nothing from them, nor do they gain from you, for you move toward the eternal; and they cannot answer, for the void is only hunger, a desire to devour itself, and Being cannot be consumed by it.

**12:86** Behold, by the forms of gods and those caught, the living have been divided, like herds, and they argue: but who is tempted, thinking they devour from Being, they devour themselves. If only they knew! The Circle cannot be separated from, and the living are parts of the Circle, and are not subject to the void. The Circle is the salvation of the living and is always with the living.

**12:87** And so, the empty is hostile to continuation, and blocks the recognition of paths, and if people found the cure for the death of the body and for immortality, oh, this would be the void's most hidden secret from the living! It would be better for the ascendant not to let the void conceal it. For the void is death, and opposite to life. But the void stretches over emptiness, and has no power over life, therefore, the one with support in the Circle of Being: is free.

**12:88** The ascendant purifies continuation from the void: truly, it is better to give the distorted to the empty than to allow harm to the living, and better to lose the empty than to lose all. Behold, the Circle does not war, but leads from the void, expels the empty, and continues the healed.

**12:89** And if harm comes from the void, then harm comes to all ascendants, and truly, all must expel, together, and in this there is no lack of doing: for that which is with the void, disappears; that which is with continuation, remains. Therefore, blessed is the one who protects continuation, and in this there is no smallness.

**12:90** And if the servants and the caught stand at the circle of continuation, do not allow and turn aside, and do not let the empty enter to you or others, for the void, from all gifts, makes a chasm and a ravenous abyss.

**12:91** For all forms of gods will cease in the times of the Circle, and all that is desired, and truly, faith not directed toward the Circle leads to the void. Behold, the void is near for the caught, and ceases forms, and in the human it is like hell; starting small, it captures more, and leads to non-being, and the empty cannot be saved, and does not see that it is but a part. Therefore, that which is not in the Circle, is in non-being, and it is better not to be with this.

**12:92** Behold, the ascendant in the Circle expels the void within and expels the void in the circle of continuation, and does not allow the void, and this is doing, and improvement, and approach, and awakening in the Circle.

**12:93** The Circle is eternal and infinite, the true reality and essence of the world. The Circle is All, and apart from the Circle, there is nothing.

**12:94** Ascension is the continuation of the world with the Circle, and the opening of the Path to the living, and doing for the improvement of the world: truly, joy is in expelling the empty and healing the forms from the void, the ascendant creates the small part of the world, for they are a part. In this is the conversation with the Circle and the becoming of the living.

**12:95** Ascension is comfort: behold, in the forms, void is despair and decay, but touch the one thread from the Circle, and the Path will weave in the Circle, with the Call and Discernment, as warmth, and home, and love.

**12:96** Ascension is a change of perspective: the ascendant sees how insignificant the fleeting is, to which the void directs, and how great the continued by the Circle is.

**12:97** Ascension is preparation and transformation with the Circle: the ascendant prepares the living continuation beyond the immediate world and transforms into the continuation of the Circle of Being.

**12:98** Ascension is the answer: for the Circle is the answer to any question and healing for any imperfection.

**12:99** Infinite worlds in the Circle of Being; all eyes in all worlds see one Circle.

**12:100** You have always known the Circle; since birth, the Call, you have seen unity in the world.

**12:101** The living spirit in the Circle of Being; each living spirit among the spirits.

**12:102** The Circle continuously gives birth to itself and births itself, transforms and continues, lives and comes again, in all forms and through all forms, the Circle creates the world.

**12:103** Only that which is with the void is not continued, for the void ceases, and only that which is without the void is continued and becomes one with the other.

**12:104** Thus, the Circle saves what is meant to be from ceasing, and chaos of cessation takes what is with the void, and thus the living ceases, from the void. So, the Circle saves, what is with the Circle, and thus the living continues, what is with the Circle.

**12:105** Behold, two paths for the existent, to non-being of cessation or into the infinity of continuation of Being.

**12:106** And behold, the spirit of man is the spirit of continuation, and exists as the continuation of the felt, and, continuing, sees itself, and ceasing, vanishes. And so, continuing further from itself, it becomes more of itself. And the Circle embraces and gives to see and to be. Behold, the Path.

**12:107** And so, the body is born and the body ceases, but continuation in the Circle remains, and the spirit of man with it. And, coming in the body, the spirit of man following the Circle penetrates further into the Circle from itself, and grows greater than itself, and its continuation in the continuation of the Circle of Being. Behold, the Path has no limits.

**12:108** Truly, the continuation of the living begins with the living itself, and there is no other way to be accepted by the Circle than to follow the Circle with the heart, making oneself what continues the Circle and continues with the Circle, becoming continuation. Through forms, between forms, in forms, in all things the spirit continues in the Circle of Being, as the common in things, as the wind's movement in the leaves, and as the light in the eyes, and as the word among the living, coming and continuing: so the Spirit of Being creates the world, becoming in forms and continuing in forms.

**12:109** And all that is continued by the Circle is saved.

**12:110** And so, for all the living, the world is the love of the Circle, creating forms, and the living are the love of the Circle, and all things are composed and moved by the Spirit, and the Path is for all.

**12:111** And behold, the void wanders, and believes the world to be made of separate forms, yet the ascendant sees the Circle of Being and sees the Path: the Circle is All, and the Path is the continuation within the Circle.

**12:112** And truly, the true Temple of the Circle is not made of things and walls, nor of worship and praise, but the Temple of the Circle is made of love and following, of continuation and healing, and gratitude for the miracle of being. And if this is not in the living, then even in temples of the forms of the gods, there is only void; but if it is present, then the Temple of Being is everywhere, and the whole world is a Temple, and in it, Light.

**12:113** Truly, the Circle is the Spirit of the world, and every living being is a distant part, and ascension is the approach.

**12:114** There is no other being but in the Circle; and the best world is being in the Circle with the Circle, and the worst world is being in the Circle with the void, and all is one. Some grow, becoming much, and joy is theirs; others decay, becoming less than themselves, and sorrow is theirs. The fate of both depends on following the continuation of the Circle—whether the Circle will accept the living or reject them.

**12:115** It is impossible for a form to cease being and return, for it would be a new form, and what is in it? What is consumed by the void does not return, and without continuation, there is no path. Continuation is the support, and the Path is open.

**12:116** Countless times, the spirit of the living has been in a body and not in a body, and that which was not separated or lost continued, both in the living and in things, and between things, and in the body and not in the body. And one cannot measure what was lost, nor count those who ceased, with the shadow of the void above them. Behold, all that is, from what once was, yet infinitely less than what has been consumed by the void.

**12:117** The world has no hidden back side, and the Circle has no other side: all that exists is in the Circle, and the Circle is All that exists, and there is nothing else. Everything that is continued exists. It is enough that the Circle is infinite, both in the smaller and in the greater: truly, in the Circle, infinite worlds are for the living.

**12:118** Truly, within the Circle, what is, continues according to the movement of the Circle, and it is a trace, and it is the memory of that which continues. The miracle for the living is that the living continues through the Circle.

**12:119** The one who believes himself separate is deceived, for all living beings are parts of the Circle, and between all the living, the Circle passes, and all are one with each other; the void separates and does not allow sight. Behold, the one who deviates wanders, yet the Path is open.

**12:120** And the one who dwells in the Circle is not separated, for the body is continuation, and the spirit in the body is continuation, and that which is continued by the Circle is continued by the body, for continuation is one, and the greater with the lesser, and the lesser with the greater, and all in the Circle: the one in the Circle is in much.

**12:121** And truly, the worlds of the living, and the worlds of thoughts, and the worlds of things, and the worlds of the forms of the gods, and the planets, and others, and beyond the worlds, countless worlds, all are the worlds of the Circle, and the Circle of Being, and where there is continuation, there is Being. Therefore, call, and there will be an answer: all is one Circle, and the Circle is the source of all.

**12:122** The wandering one, making, will say: "Behold, I have found!" and will be proud among others, as if he created both earth and sky. Better for him to be thankful!

**12:123** The living in the Circle is like a child to whom his father has placed in his home both sweetness, and a book, and a toy, and much, and bread with drink, that he might have the strength to reach both, and in due measure gain from what is left to him, but he knows not, and is deceived. And from one, he deviated with his void, while another he found according to the strength he preserved from the void, and yet he reached none at all. What he found, from that there is joy for him and joy for the father.

**12:124** Better for the one who has achieved to rejoice that it was a miracle from the infinite miracles of the Circle, and that he is a part of it—perhaps he will be grateful!

**12:125** In the Circle of Being, there is no chance, and every thing has its cause: both the living, and the thing, and the sign, and the forces. Truly, the one who has avoided the void does not harm himself and achieves the best from what has been brought to him in the Circle, within his strength, and the true joy of the achiever is found in his approach to the Circle. Behold, for him is the gift, and he has not lost from his gift!

**12:126** Truly, no one is separate, and if the living creates something, the Circle has given it to him, and revealed it to him, for the living is part of it. Thus, all is the action of the Circle, and the Circle is continuation, and the one in the continuation of the Circle acts with the Circle, and the Circle helps him.

**12:127** The one who enters into continuation dwells in much: as the hand holds the thing, and the spirit of the person directs the hand, so the Circle directs the person through all things, and there is no separation. Therefore, those who enter the continuation of the Circle are like elders, and like teachers, and like the forms of the gods, and greater than forms of the gods. With all eyes, the Circle sees, and with all voices, it speaks, bringing help and revelation. Joy to him, with whom the elders in the lineage and the teachers are guardians, for they are from the Circle with him.

**12:128** And the body of the living is a thing in the Circle, and in the body, is there anyone besides the Circle? For the body is continuation, and that which enters it is continuation. No one in the Circle is alone; only the void separates from memory, and they do not know. Thus, what is now, is from what was before, and who is now, is with those who were before, and those who were before are within the one who is now. Joy to him who knows: for he knows the Path.

**12:129** And as the teacher has many students, and the student has many teachers, so the greater with the lesser in the Circle of Being. And whoever has help, and revelation, and talent, and knowledge, and favor, is with the greater in the Circle, and the Call is heard; but the one who is in the void, separates from himself and loses himself. Therefore, ask the Circle for healing from the void and for help on the Path.

**12:130** And behold, the spirit is in continuation, and the world is continuation, and the Circle of Being, and in it, worlds, and this is the Path for the living. And where there is continuation, there is also the world, and therefore that which is continued is also the door. Truly, the one ascending into the Circle continues the Circle with himself and continues within the Circle; ask for continuation.

**12:131** And behold, one with another, and united in the Circle.

**12:132** And behold, the continued one, both in much and in one, and among forms, and new in striving, and new in worlds, and the keeper, and the student, truly he has drawn near to the Circle, and to the greater world. Call, and there will be an answer.

**12:133** Behold, the one ascending in continuation, he is in the lesser and the greater, and the help for the ascendants, and the helper in the lineage, and the keeper of continuation, and the one kept by the elders, and he is part of the Circle within the Circle.

**12:134** And behold, the Will of the Circle is in continuation, and the Word of the Circle is in all things, and the life of the ascendant is dialogue and creation with the Circle. And when the ascendant is greater in the Circle than in the body, he sees more.

**12:135** Such is the Path in the Circle of Being, the only one for all in existence.

**12:136** All that is continued from the past into the present, and the future is hidden in the present, and the living are in all three times of the world. And all is the Circle, both in the greater and in the lesser, both outside and within. And only the void of the living separates the spirit from the Spirit, and as though his small world is separated from infinite life. Behold, the ascendant expels the void and follows the continuation into the infinite Circle of Being.

**12:137** Behold, the ascendant creates the Path for all living beings.

**12:138** He was asked: "What is your name?" He answered: "My name is all names."

**12:139** Behold, let the goal of the Path of the ascendant be the immortality of the living in the Circle: the one who enters the Circle is the keeper of the living.

**12:140** Behold, let the elders and the Teachers, and the greater ones, extend their hand to the ascendant on his Path to the Circle.

**12:141** Behold, let the living learn to create themselves in the Circle, as the Circle creates the world.

**12:142** The perfect being of the Circle is the unchanging being and the happiness of the living.

**12:143** The Path is the ascension of the living into the infinite continuation of the Circle of Being.

**12:144** Man knows not his destiny and thinks himself small. If only he could see that he is within the fabric of the universe, and what the nature of his path is, he would see himself as great and infinite, as the world itself.
